# Design context

Captured from the founding interview, not inferred from the code. Correct anything
that has drifted — this file is what design work is calibrated against.

## Who uses it

Roughly 38 people from one WhatsApp group who play casual 9-a-side football at
Zandvlei Sports Ground in Muizenberg, Cape Town. Mixed ages and abilities. Almost
entirely on phones, often on patchy data, frequently outdoors and often at dusk.

They are being asked to install Telegram *for this*, which is the whole design
problem: the app has to be worth a new messenger. The fantasy league is the carrot.

## What they are trying to do

In rough order of how often it happens:

1. Find out whether there is a game this week, and which night.
2. Say yes or no, in one tap, without reading a thread.
3. See the teams once they are picked, and where the pitch is.
4. Report what they did afterwards — goals, assists, nutmegs, tackles, saves, and a
   vote for man of the match.
5. Look at their own card, the table, and argue about both.

Nobody is doing admin. There is no organiser and no committee: the bot is the
organiser, the group votes, and the game happens unless nobody turns up.

## How it should feel

- **Honest.** Every number is self-reported and unverified, and the interface says
  so rather than implying a system of record. No fake precision.
- **Nobody in charge.** No admin panels, no approvals, no "pending" states.
- **A real league, lightly worn.** It should look like a proper football league —
  clean tables, confident numbers, a table you would screenshot — without the
  self-importance of one. The joke is that a group of mates has a Hall of Fame.
- **Muizenberg.** The bathing boxes on the beach are the one image everybody has of
  this place: a row of small huts, each a different flat bright colour, on pale
  sand. That is the identity. Not surf clichés, not palm trees.

## Non-negotiables

- Phone first. The table is read one-handed, outdoors, in sunlight.
- The Telegram screens and the website must look like one product. People arrive at
  the site by tapping a picture in a chat.
- No club name exists yet. Do not invent one.
- Less is more. Clear and simple beats atmospheric.
